Machine-readable immutable input records including each source SHA256 and
Git blob are maintained in apps/rinne/src/review-motion-sources.js.
Generated simulator/assets/review-motions/manifest.json records the exact
source repository, commit, path, clip name/index, author, license, hash,
compiled payload hash and canonical identity for every registered motion.
Generated audit.json preserves all duplicate/variant mappings and rejected
clips with reasons. Runtime payloads are immutable-source verified and lazy-loaded on selection.
