## Portrait UI revision (0.3)

The RPGUI/Buch pixel-art frames, buttons, gauges and icons have been removed from the live entry. The dedicated portrait layout and CSS treatments are original UI under the user's explicit UI-only exception of 2026-09-12. Character/environment/animation restrictions remain unchanged.

Icons are sourced from Lucide at immutable commit a79b2d131dab2bf20cb224bd0937b439a9c4fa99. Full ISC and Feather-derived MIT notices are in [LUCIDE_LICENSE.md](./LUCIDE_LICENSE.md). Display color/size are adapted via CSS, not redrawn.

## Preview limits

This source preview is not a complete commercial release. Audio replacement, distinct weapon art for every profession, detailed face/hair editing, online features, self-hosted audited asset bundles and custom-domain production publication remain incomplete. The game contains no procedural primitive model, handwritten SVG illustration or synthesized-audio replacement path. Generic math planes are used only for pointer intersection, never as visible art.
