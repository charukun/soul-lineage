Acquisition in this package:
The vertex positions, triangle indices and material groups of six existing OBJ
files were retrieved through GitHub and converted to indexed JSON. Unused OBJ
UVs and explicit normals were removed; renderer normals are recalculated.
The exterior facility meshes are assemblies of these supplied modular asset
meshes, not newly modelled primitives. The stock geometry is NOT a felt model.
Procedural textile surface detail is a rendering adaptation added in this demo.
Original vertices and face index order are preserved in the conversion.
See assets/kenney-converted.json for individual source URLs and blob SHAs.
