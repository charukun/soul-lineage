# 百年転生: 水底の一族問答

## Accepted experience

A new local family begins in a quiet underwater memory scene, before birth. Three short, interactive questions choose a cultural homeland, a family ethos, and an heirloom tradition. The scene uses water light, drifting memories, pointer ripples, and a soul glow rather than a web form. The final preview shows the family crest, home, family silhouettes, and heirloom. The explicit action is 「この家に、生まれる」. Answers can be revisited before confirmation.

Existing Continue resumes the saved person and family without repeating the questions or resetting progress. Cancelling a new-family journey never overwrites the existing life. Save-less Continue must not silently create a random family. Confirming a replacement life requires an explicit overwrite acknowledgement. Existing v2 saves migrate without forced onboarding or loss of age, skills, village, equipment, or ancestry.

## Family contract

- Culture, ethos, heirloom, and a stable family identity are saved with the life, not in a separate preference that can drift from the save.
- Rebirth retains the family and records each generation's contributions. A family tradition is not a weapon lock or a combat-stat lottery.
- Japanese mountain-house / katana is one supported combination. Other choices are plains / woodland homes and spear / staff traditions, mapped to existing supported weapons rather than inventing unusable equipment.
- The family's crest, home memory, lore, and inherited practice remain visible on the return title and family memory surface. Childhood observes and learns; existing weapon access at age seven remains unchanged.
- Birth village selection, existing world layout, movement, rest, tutorial, combat rules, and other apps are preserved.

## Acceptance and validation

Focused native tests cover all question combinations, canonical validation, save round-trip and legacy migration, stable inheritance, family contributions, no weapon restriction, and cancel/confirm/re-entry behavior. A rinne build verifies production integration. Browser evidence uses the repository's existing browser playtest/evidence route when required. No persistent Actions job, step, broad test sweep, or build lifecycle expansion is introduced.
